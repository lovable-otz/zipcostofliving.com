ZipCost is a free cost-of-living and moving toolkit that compares states and metro areas with the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis Regional Price Parities, plus rent and moving calculators.

Compare places

  • Cost of Living Calculator
    Compare the cost of living between any two states or metro areas and see the salary you need to keep the same buying power, from BEA data.
  • Salary Comparison by City
    See what your salary is worth in another metro area once local prices are counted, for 380+ U.S. metros using BEA price levels.

Renting

  • Rent Affordability Calculator
    Find the rent you can afford from your income with the 30% guideline and the 40× landlord income test, and adjust both to your situation.
  • Prorated Rent Calculator
    Calculate partial-month rent for a move-in or move-out date three ways: actual days in the month, a 365-day year or a 30-day month.
  • Rent Split Calculator
    Split rent between roommates equally, by income or by bedroom size with a shared-space share, and see each person’s monthly amount.
  • Rent vs Buy Calculator
    Compare renting with buying over the years you plan to stay, including closing and selling costs, upkeep and the return on money you keep.

Moving

  • Moving Cost Calculator

  • Budget Calculator (50/30/20)

Where the numbers come from

Every price comparison on ZipCost uses the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis’s Regional Price Parities for 2024, released February 19, 2026, which compare price levels across all 50 states, the District of Columbia and 387 metro areas. The comparisons cover states and metro areas, not individual ZIP codes. The moving calculator’s default fuel price is the U.S. Energy Information Administration’s weekly average for regular gasoline ($4.319 at launch, for the week of September 14, 2026). We refresh the price levels when BEA publishes its yearly update and the fuel price each week after EIA’s release. The guides name the publisher and date of every mover and storage price they quote. The site is maintained by ZipCost. The methodology page shows every formula.
