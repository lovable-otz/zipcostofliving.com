Privacy policy

Last updated September 20, 2026.

What you type into our tools is processed in your browser and is not sent to us. The tools do not store your entries. If you press “Copy link to this result”, your entries are placed in the copied link after the # sign so the person you share it with sees the same result; that part of a link is not sent to our server, and our analytics is set up to ignore it.

Analytics, advertising and partners

Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service from Google, to understand how the site is used, for example which pages are visited, how visitors arrive, and roughly what kind of device and region they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this information. You can block it with Google’s Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, which works in Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Microsoft Edge, or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Advertising and partners. The site shows no advertising and has no lead, referral or affiliate partners at launch. If that changes, we will update this page before any ad network or partner is added and name each one here.

Your tool inputs. The numbers you enter in the calculators, such as your salary, rent or moving quotes, are processed in your browser. They are not sent to us or to Google Analytics.
