About ZipCost

ZipCost is a free cost-of-living and moving toolkit that compares states and metro areas with the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis Regional Price Parities, plus rent and moving calculators.

This site exists to answer the money questions that come with a move: whether a salary will go as far somewhere else, how much rent your income supports, what the first partial month will cost, how to split rent fairly, whether to rent or buy, and how much cash the move itself needs. Each tool shows its working, so you can check the math instead of taking a single number on trust.

The site is run by ZipCost. Cost-of-living comparisons use the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis’s Regional Price Parities, which cover states and metro areas. We don’t publish ZIP-code-level price data. The moving tool’s fuel default comes from the U.S. Energy Information Administration. Rules of thumb such as the 30% rent guideline, the 40× landlord income test and the 50/30/20 budget are labeled as guidelines, and you can change them in each tool. The methodology page lists every formula and source.

The results are estimates for general information. They are not financial, tax, legal or real estate advice, and your lease, your lender and your written quotes decide the real numbers.

Corrections. If you find a wrong figure, an outdated source or a calculation that doesn’t match its description, please get in touch through the contact page with the page address and what you found. We check the claim against the original source, fix confirmed errors, and update the page’s date. Price data is refreshed when BEA and EIA publish new figures.
