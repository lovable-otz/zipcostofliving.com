Cost of Living in West Virginia

Prices in West Virginia were 10.5% below U.S. average in 2024, by the BEA Regional Price Parities (U.S. = 100; West Virginia = 89.497). Housing stood at 54.241. To match a $60,000 salary at average U.S. prices, you would need about $53,698 in West Virginia.
