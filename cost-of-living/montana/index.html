Cost of Living in Montana

Prices in Montana were 5.4% below U.S. average in 2024, by the BEA Regional Price Parities (U.S. = 100; Montana = 94.645). Housing stood at 84.648. To match a $60,000 salary at average U.S. prices, you would need about $56,787 in Montana.
