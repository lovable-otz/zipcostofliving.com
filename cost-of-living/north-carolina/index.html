What makes North Carolina cheaper or more expensive?

North Carolina prices were 5.7% below the U.S. average in BEA’s 2024 Regional Price Parities (94.326, rank 31 of 51). Rents make the biggest difference at 18.6% below average (81.364), and utilities are 11.4% cheaper (88.619). Goods (96.621) and other services (98.186) are only a little under the norm.

The Census Bureau’s 2024 American Community Survey puts the median gross rent at $1,338 a month (U.S.: $1,487) and the median owner-occupied home value at $333,000 (U.S.: $360,600). Both come in under the national medians.

No North Carolina metro reaches the national average. Raleigh-Cary is the most expensive at 98.157, and matching a $60,000 salary there takes about $58,894. Rocky Mount is the least expensive at 88.012, needing about $52,807, a gap of $6,087. Durham-Chapel Hill and Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia sit just behind, between 97 and 98. Asheville and Wilmington are near 96.5. A group of eastern and foothill metros, including Greenville, Goldsboro, Hickory and Pinehurst-Southern Pines, sits near 88. Charlotte’s metro extends into South Carolina, and the Virginia Beach metro listed here is mostly in Virginia. Compare an offer across these areas with the salary comparison by city.

What should movers to North Carolina budget for?

North Carolina taxes wages at a flat rate, has a mid-range sales tax, and ties the deposit cap to the length of your lease.

  • Income tax: a flat 3.99% for 2026, per the Tax Foundation (data as of February 11, 2026).
  • Sales tax: a 4.75% state rate plus a 2.35% average local rate, 7.10% combined, the 22nd highest combined rate in the country as of July 1, 2026, per the Tax Foundation.
  • Security deposit: the North Carolina Real Estate Commission (November 2025) summarizes the Tenant Security Deposit Act. The maximum is two weeks’ rent for a week-to-week tenancy, one and one-half months’ rent for month-to-month, and two months’ rent for longer leases. The landlord must refund the deposit or send an itemized accounting within 30 days after the tenancy ends, with a final accounting no later than 60 days if damage takes longer to assess.
  • Car: the NCDMV 2024 fee adjustment schedule (revised May 9, 2024) sets the title application fee at $66.75, before registration fees and taxes.

What drives the cost of living in North Carolina

The headline number is an average of several categories, and they rarely move together. Services is the biggest upward pull at 98.2 (1.8% below the national level). housing pulls hardest the other way at 81.4 (18.6% below). Housing is the category that moves a household budget most, and in North Carolina it runs 18.6% below the U.S. level, so rent or mortgage is the main reason the overall figure sits where it does.

Because the categories are weighted by how much a typical household spends, a large gap in a small category moves the overall figure less than a modest gap in housing. That is why two states with the same headline RPP can feel quite different to live in.

Where in North Carolina is cheapest?

State averages hide a lot. Among the 16 metro areas the BEA publishes for North Carolina, Raleigh-Cary, NC is the most expensive at 98.2 and Rocky Mount, NC the least at 88.0 — a spread of 10.1 points. Rural areas outside these metros are typically cheaper still for housing, though utilities and fuel can offset part of that.
