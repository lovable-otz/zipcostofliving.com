What makes New York cheaper or more expensive?

New York prices were 7.9% above the U.S. average in BEA’s 2024 Regional Price Parities (107.921), the 5th most expensive of 51. Utilities are the steepest category at 34.4% above average (134.37), followed by rents at 22.2% above (122.168). Goods run 7.3% above and other services 4.1% above, so almost every part of the budget costs more.

The Census Bureau’s 2024 American Community Survey puts the median gross rent at $1,634 a month (U.S.: $1,487) and the median owner-occupied home value at $449,800 (U.S.: $360,600).

The statewide figure is pulled up by one metro. New York-Newark-Jersey City scores 112.563, and matching a $60,000 salary there takes about $67,538. At the other end, Watertown-Fort Drum scores 87.69, where the same standard of living needs about $52,614, a gap of $14,924. Upstate metros such as Rochester (97.035), Buffalo-Cheektowaga (95.844) and Syracuse (95.742) all sit below the national average. Ithaca is an outlier: its housing figure (128.377) is higher than Kiryas Joel-Poughkeepsie-Newburgh’s, but its overall index is lower. Because the New York City metro crosses into New Jersey, its figure covers both sides of the Hudson. Use the salary comparison by city to see what an offer is worth in each area.

What should movers to New York budget for?

New York taxes wages, splits sales tax roughly evenly between state and local levels, and caps deposits at one month’s rent.

  • Income tax: New York uses a graduated scale from 3.9% up to 10.9% on single filers’ income over $25,000,000, per the Tax Foundation (data as of February 11, 2026).
  • Sales tax: a 4.00% state rate plus a 4.54% average local rate, 8.54% combined, the 10th highest combined rate in the country as of July 1, 2026, per the Tax Foundation. The local share is larger than the state share.
  • Security deposit: General Obligations Law section 7-108 says a deposit or advance may not exceed one month’s rent, with narrow exceptions. Within 14 days after you vacate, the landlord must give an itemized statement and return the rest, or lose the right to keep any of it.
  • Car and license: the NY DMV requires new residents to register a vehicle within 30 days, and its moving guide sets the same 30 days for a New York driver license (checked September 16, 2026). Sales tax is collected at registration.
