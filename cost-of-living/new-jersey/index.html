Cost of Living in New Jersey

Prices in New Jersey were 8.8% above U.S. average in 2024, by the BEA Regional Price Parities (U.S. = 100; New Jersey = 108.805). Housing stood at 134.292. To match a $60,000 salary at average U.S. prices, you would need about $65,283 in New Jersey.
