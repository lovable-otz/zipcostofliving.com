Cost of Living in New Mexico

Prices in New Mexico were 7.8% below U.S. average in 2024, by the BEA Regional Price Parities (U.S. = 100; New Mexico = 92.212). Housing stood at 73.611. To match a $60,000 salary at average U.S. prices, you would need about $55,327 in New Mexico.
