Cost of Living in Arkansas

Prices in Arkansas were 13.1% below U.S. average in 2024, by the BEA Regional Price Parities (U.S. = 100; Arkansas = 86.937). Housing stood at 58.203. To match a $60,000 salary at average U.S. prices, you would need about $52,162 in Arkansas.
