Cost of Living in Indiana

Prices in Indiana were 6.7% below U.S. average in 2024, by the BEA Regional Price Parities (U.S. = 100; Indiana = 93.329). Housing stood at 73.926. To match a $60,000 salary at average U.S. prices, you would need about $55,997 in Indiana.
