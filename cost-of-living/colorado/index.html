What makes Colorado cheaper or more expensive?

Colorado prices were 3.1% above the U.S. average in BEA’s 2024 Regional Price Parities (103.052, rank 13 of 51), and housing explains all of it. Rents stand 27.4% above average (127.359). Goods (98.727) and other services (99.613) are slightly below 100, and utilities are 15% cheaper than average (84.971).

The Census Bureau’s 2024 American Community Survey puts the median gross rent at $1,822 a month (U.S.: $1,487) and the median owner-occupied home value at $574,600 (U.S.: $360,600). Both are well above the national medians.

The Front Range carries the cost. Denver-Aurora-Centennial is the most expensive metro at 105.782, and Boulder follows at 105.202 with the state’s highest housing figure (157.02). Matching a $60,000 salary in Denver takes about $63,469. Pueblo is the least expensive at 91.762, needing about $55,057, a gap of $8,412. Grand Junction on the Western Slope (95.476) also runs below average. Fort Collins-Loveland, Colorado Springs and Greeley sit just above 100. Mountain resort towns are not broken out, because BEA does not publish metro figures for areas outside metro areas. What should movers to Colorado budget for?

Colorado has a flat income tax, a local-heavy sales tax, a legal cap on deposits and a longer car registration window than most states.

  • Income tax: a flat 4.40%, per the Tax Foundation (data as of February 11, 2026).
  • Sales tax: a 2.90% state rate plus a 4.99% average local rate, 7.89% combined, the 16th highest combined rate in the country as of July 1, 2026, per the Tax Foundation. Local taxes are higher than the state rate, so your total depends on where you live.
  • Security deposit: SB23-184, effective August 7, 2023, bars landlords from requiring a deposit above two months’ rent.
  • Car: the Colorado DMV gives new residents 90 days to title and register a vehicle, in person at the county motor vehicle office. The largest part of the cost is the Specific Ownership Tax. Late registration costs $25 per month, up to $100, and older vehicles in the designated area need an emissions test (checked September 16, 2026).
