Cost of Living in Nebraska

Prices in Nebraska were 9.9% below U.S. average in 2024, by the BEA Regional Price Parities (U.S. = 100; Nebraska = 90.103). Housing stood at 75.174. To match a $60,000 salary at average U.S. prices, you would need about $54,062 in Nebraska.
