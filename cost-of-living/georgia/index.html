What makes Georgia cheaper or more expensive?

Georgia prices were 3.7% below the U.S. average in BEA’s 2024 Regional Price Parities (96.293, rank 27 of 51). Rents do most of the work at 11.3% below average (88.694). Utilities are 7.2% cheaper (92.814), while goods (98.929) and other services (97.343) are close to the national level.

The Census Bureau’s 2024 American Community Survey puts the median gross rent at $1,506 a month (U.S.: $1,487) and the median owner-occupied home value at $343,300 (U.S.: $360,600). Rent is almost identical to the national median, while home values are a little lower.

Atlanta-Sandy Springs-Roswell is the most expensive Georgia metro and sits right at the national average (100.058), with housing at 111.022. Matching a $60,000 salary there takes about $60,035. Albany is the least expensive at 87.669, needing about $52,601, a gap of $7,434. Gainesville (96.766) and Savannah (95.206) come next after Atlanta. South Georgia metros such as Valdosta, Brunswick-St. Simons and Macon-Bibb County sit near 88. Augusta, Columbus and Chattanooga all cross state lines, so their figures include parts of South Carolina, Alabama or Tennessee. What should movers to Georgia budget for?

Georgia taxes wages at a flat rate that dropped in 2026. It also caps deposits and charges new residents a one-time tax on their cars.

  • Income tax: a flat 4.99% beginning January 1, 2026, down from 5.19%, under HB 463, per the Governor’s office (May 11, 2026) and the Georgia Department of Revenue.
  • Sales tax: a 4.00% state rate plus a 3.56% average local rate, 7.56% combined, the 18th highest combined rate in the country as of July 1, 2026, per the Tax Foundation.
  • Security deposit: the Safe at Home Act (HB 404) added Code section 44-7-30.1, which bars a deposit above two months’ rent. It applies to leases entered into or renewed on or after July 1, 2024.
  • Car: the Georgia Department of Revenue requires registration within 30 days of moving. New residents pay a Title Ad Valorem Tax of 3% of the vehicle’s fair market value, plus an $18 title fee and a $20 annual registration fee (checked September 16, 2026). On a car worth $20,000, that tax alone is $600.

What drives the cost of living in Georgia

The headline number is an average of several categories, and they rarely move together. Goods is the biggest upward pull at 98.9 (1.1% below the national level). housing pulls hardest the other way at 88.7 (11.3% below). Housing is the category that moves a household budget most, and in Georgia it runs 11.3% below the U.S. level, so rent or mortgage is the main reason the overall figure sits where it does.

Because the categories are weighted by how much a typical household spends, a large gap in a small category moves the overall figure less than a modest gap in housing. That is why two states with the same headline RPP can feel quite different to live in.

Where in Georgia is cheapest?

State averages hide a lot. Among the 15 metro areas the BEA publishes for Georgia, Atlanta-Sandy Springs-Roswell, GA is the most expensive at 100.1 and Albany, GA the least at 87.7 — a spread of 12.4 points. Rural areas outside these metros are typically cheaper still for housing, though utilities and fuel can offset part of that.
