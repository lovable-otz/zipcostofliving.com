What makes California cheaper or more expensive?

California is the most expensive state in BEA’s 2024 Regional Price Parities: 110.72 against a U.S. average of 100, rank 1 of 51. Utilities (158.899) and rents (154.346) do the damage. Goods sit only 6.1% above average and other services 2.6%, so the premium comes mostly from keeping a roof over your head and the lights on.

The Census Bureau’s 2024 American Community Survey puts the median gross rent at $2,104 a month (U.S.: $1,487) and the median owner-occupied home value at $759,500 (U.S.: $360,600). Both run far above the national figures, which is what the housing index is picking up.

Where you land matters. San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont tops the state at 115.613, while El Centro sits at 95.174, below the U.S. average. That 20-point gap means matching a $60,000 salary takes about $69,368 in the Bay Area but $57,104 in El Centro, a $12,264 difference. San Jose has the highest housing figure in the table (211.901) even though its overall index is lower than San Francisco’s. Inland metros such as Fresno, Bakersfield-Delano and Merced come in close to or under 100 overall. These are area averages; test your own numbers in the cost of living calculator.

What should movers to California budget for?

Plan for a state income tax, a high sales tax, a capped deposit and a quick car registration deadline.

  • Income tax: California taxes wages on a graduated scale from 1% up to a top rate of 13.3% on single filers’ income over $1,000,000, per the Tax Foundation (data as of February 11, 2026).
  • Sales tax: a 7.25% state rate plus a 1.78% average local rate, 9.03% combined, the 7th highest combined rate in the country as of July 1, 2026, per the Tax Foundation.
  • Security deposit: under Civil Code section 1950.5 (as amended effective January 1, 2026), most landlords may take no more than one month’s rent as security, on top of the first month’s rent. Some small landlords who are individuals may take up to two months. The landlord must return what is owed, with an itemized statement, within 21 days after you move out.
  • Car: the California DMV says a vehicle brought in from another state must be registered within 20 days of becoming a resident (checked September 16, 2026).
