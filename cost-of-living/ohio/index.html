Cost of Living in Ohio

Prices in Ohio were 7.2% below U.S. average in 2024, by the BEA Regional Price Parities (U.S. = 100; Ohio = 92.774). Housing stood at 73.012. To match a $60,000 salary at average U.S. prices, you would need about $55,664 in Ohio.
