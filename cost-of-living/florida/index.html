BEA Regional Price Parities, 2024 (U.S. = 100)

Florida prices were 3.4% above the U.S. average in BEA’s 2024 Regional Price Parities (103.414, rank 11 of 51), and rent is the reason. The housing figure stands at 122.107, 22.1% above average, while goods (98.059) and utilities (90.074) are actually cheaper than the national norm. Other services sit just 1.3% above.

The Census Bureau’s 2024 American Community Survey puts the median gross rent at $1,812 a month (U.S.: $1,487) and the median owner-occupied home value at $396,900 (U.S.: $360,600). Rent is well above the national median, which lines up with the BEA housing figure.

Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach is the most expensive Florida metro at 114.155, with housing at 155.551. Matching a $60,000 salary there takes about $68,493. Wildwood-The Villages is the least expensive at 85.424, needing about $51,254, a gap of $17,239, wider than the spread in California or New York. Most of the Gulf and Atlantic coast metros, including Naples, Sarasota, Cape Coral, Orlando and Tampa, cluster between 100 and 104. North and inland metros such as Tallahassee, Ocala and Sebring run below 96. Check how a specific rent fits your pay with the rent affordability calculator.

What should movers to Florida budget for?

Florida has no wage tax, a lower-than-average combined sales tax and a one-time fee for bringing a car into the state.

  • Income tax: Florida is one of eight states with no individual income tax, per the Tax Foundation (as of February 11, 2026).
  • Sales tax: a 6.00% state rate plus a 0.98% average local rate, 6.98% combined, the 28th highest combined rate in the country as of July 1, 2026, per the Tax Foundation.
  • Security deposit: the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services summary of Chapter 83, Florida Statutes, says a landlord who is not making a claim must return the deposit within 15 days. Otherwise the landlord has 30 days to send written notice of how much will be kept and why (checked September 16, 2026).
  • Car: the Orange County Tax Collector says new residents must register vehicles within 10 days of establishing residency. The state charges a $225 initial registration fee if you have never had a Florida plate to transfer (checked September 16, 2026).

Add the $225 fee to your total in the moving cost calculator.

What drives the cost of living in Florida

The headline number is an average of several categories, and they rarely move together. Housing is the biggest upward pull at 122.1 (22.1% above the national level). utilities pulls hardest the other way at 90.1 (9.9% below). Housing is the category that moves a household budget most, and in Florida it runs 22.1% above the U.S. level, so rent or mortgage is the main reason the overall figure sits where it does.

Because the categories are weighted by how much a typical household spends, a large gap in a small category moves the overall figure less than a modest gap in housing. That is why two states with the same headline RPP can feel quite different to live in.

Where in Florida is cheapest?

State averages hide a lot. Among the 22 metro areas the BEA publishes for Florida, Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach, FL is the most expensive at 114.2 and Wildwood-The Villages, FL the least at 85.4 — a spread of 28.7 points. Rural areas outside these metros are typically cheaper still for housing, though utilities and fuel can offset part of that.
