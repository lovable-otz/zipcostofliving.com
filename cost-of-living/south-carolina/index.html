Cost of Living in South Carolina

Prices in South Carolina were 6.3% below U.S. average in 2024, by the BEA Regional Price Parities (U.S. = 100; South Carolina = 93.749). Housing stood at 80.49. To match a $60,000 salary at average U.S. prices, you would need about $56,249 in South Carolina.
