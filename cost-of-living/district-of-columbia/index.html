Cost of Living in District of Columbia

Prices in District of Columbia were 9.9% above U.S. average in 2024, by the BEA Regional Price Parities (U.S. = 100; District of Columbia = 109.901). Housing stood at 154.984. To match a $60,000 salary at average U.S. prices, you would need about $65,941 in District of Columbia.

What drives the cost of living in District of Columbia

The headline number is an average of several categories, and they rarely move together. Housing is the biggest upward pull at 155.0 (55.0% above the national level). services pulls hardest the other way at 103.0 (3.0% above). Housing is the category that moves a household budget most, and in District of Columbia it runs 55.0% above the U.S. level, so rent or mortgage is the main reason the overall figure sits where it does.

Because the categories are weighted by how much a typical household spends, a large gap in a small category moves the overall figure less than a modest gap in housing. That is why two states with the same headline RPP can feel quite different to live in.
